Tierstrum is the software company built on Helix. It is the commercial entity under which
products are designed, built, and sold. A working codebase exists at
`~/Documents/projects/tierstrum` (Next.js). This document is its business and product
roadmap.

---

## Vision

Tierstrum builds premium software products on one mature, reusable foundation. Each product
reuses the same design system and engineering discipline but carries its own brand. The
company's advantage is compounding: every product ships faster and more polished than the
last because the foundation (Helix) and the operating practices (this roadmap directory)
are already in place.

## Brand

- Distinct from any single product; Tierstrum is the house, not a tenant.
- Built on Helix, with its own theme (colour, type, motion, density, voice).
- Brand rules and voice to be defined; align with [../BRAND_GUIDELINES.md](../BRAND_GUIDELINES.md)
  as the pattern, not the content.

## Website

- Public marketing site presenting the company and its portfolio.
- Built on Helix primitives with a Tierstrum theme.
- Clear paths: portfolio, case studies, contact/sales.

## Marketing

- Positioning: a studio that ships enterprise-grade products on a proven system.
- Content: case studies from real products (Osieri first).
- Channels and cadence to be defined once the site is live.

## Portfolio

- Osieri / CYTOLAB as the flagship reference.
- 12 Circle Fitness as the second product, demonstrating the framework across domains.
- Each portfolio entry links to its roadmap in this directory.

## Case studies

- Osieri: building a pathology operating system on Helix; the design-system freeze; the
  experience and feedback discipline.
- Reusable narrative: how one foundation produces distinct products quickly.

## Sales

- Inbound via the website initially.
- Offering: bespoke product development on the Helix foundation, plus productized SaaS over
  time.
- Qualification, pipeline, and pricing to be defined.

## Client portal

- A future authenticated surface for clients (project status, deliverables, billing).
- Built on Helix; reuses the portal patterns proven in Osieri.

## Future SaaS

- Productize the horizontal capabilities that recur across builds.
- Candidate: a hosted Helix-themed application starter for internal reuse.
- Any SaaS that stresses Helix records its gaps in [05_HELIX_v1_1.md](05_HELIX_v1_1.md).

## Business roadmap

1. Establish brand and ship the public website.
2. Publish the first case study (Osieri).
3. Stand up sales/contact and a lightweight pipeline.
4. Deliver product #2 (12 Circle) as portfolio proof and the trigger for Helix theme
   extraction.
5. Introduce a client portal and, later, productized SaaS.

Decisions affecting Tierstrum architecture are recorded in [06_DECISIONS.md](06_DECISIONS.md);
debt in [07_TECHNICAL_DEBT.md](07_TECHNICAL_DEBT.md).
